Audio Visualizer

This is a simple audio visualizer I made that reacts to music. It features three simple bar wavelengths and a sphere going around in a circle based on the music. Note that if you resize the window or it gets transformed a different way, it will look squashed and a simple page reload will fix it. If you open this in fullscreen, you'll be able to see more of it. You can upload your own audio and watch it or just press play to see the demo music. The demo music credits are below.
